# Learning (plan.md §11)

The harness teaches method until the researcher no longer needs the model:
hypothesis design, control selection, evidence quality, report writing.

## Modes

- **Guided:** coach asks for your hypothesis before showing suggestions.
- **Hints:** three levels — question, concept, concrete next observation
  (`coach.hint`, escalating only on request).
- **Independent:** no hints until close or review.
- **Review:** rubric scores with cited reasons from your actual case fields.
- **Replay:** rerun a completed lab from evidence without the earlier answer.

## Rubric (0–4, cited, never vibes)

Scope discipline · product modeling · hypothesis quality · experimental
control · impact restraint · evidence quality · alternative explanations ·
reporting · retesting · tool literacy. `coach.score` derives each from the
case record (e.g. missing `permission_ref` caps scope discipline, with the
field named). History accumulates in `mastery.jsonl`; the dashboard surfaces
the weakest skills, never request volume or finding count.

## After every case

The seven questions (§11.4: expected rule, changed variable, falsifier,
identity/ownership evidence, demonstrated vs inferred, minimum proof, next
case) are attached to every score. Advanced live-tool presets unlock on
explicit configuration + completed safety modules — never on a score alone.

## Labs

Local intentionally-vulnerable apps by default; no live target needed to
progress. Lab metadata (objective, scope, reset, expected evidence, rubric)
ships with each lab; solutions stay separate and reveal progressively.
